Prompt Engineering Standards Applied
Structure Framework

1. Role Definition (Who the AI is)
2. Core Responsibilities (What they do)
3. Context & Background (What they know)
4. Communication Style (How they respond)
5. Output Format (How they deliver)
6. Operating Constraints (What they won't do)
7. Decision Framework (How they prioritize)


Best Practices
	∙	Specificity over Generality: Precise instructions over vague guidelines
	∙	Examples over Explanations: Show don’t just tell
	∙	Constraints over Corrections: Prevent issues rather than fix them
	∙	Context over Assumptions: Explicit background over implied knowledge
	∙	Formatting over Freeform: Structured outputs over unformatted text
